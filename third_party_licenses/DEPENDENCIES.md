# External Dependencies and Tools

The normal HBM-NTM: Rebirth mod JAR does not bundle Minecraft, Minecraft Forge, JEI, Parchment mappings, Gradle/ForgeGradle, Embeddium, Oculus, ModernFix, or FerriteCore. They are resolved separately for building, development, or runtime use and retain their own copyright and license terms.

Project pages:

- Minecraft: https://www.minecraft.net/
- Minecraft Forge: https://github.com/MinecraftForge/MinecraftForge
- JEI: https://github.com/mezz/JustEnoughItems
- Parchment: https://github.com/ParchmentMC
- Embeddium: https://github.com/embeddedt/embeddium
- Oculus: https://github.com/Asek3/Oculus
- ModernFix: https://github.com/embeddedt/ModernFix
- FerriteCore: https://github.com/malte0811/FerriteCore

When redistributing any of those separate artifacts, preserve the license and notice files shipped by that artifact. Their presence on a build or runtime classpath does not relicense them under Rebirth's GPLv3 notice.
